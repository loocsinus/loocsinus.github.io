How to use this each month

  1. Check the top strip — income, expenses, net margin.
  2. Scan % of income — flag any line that jumps vs. last month.
  3. Switch to Grouped for the 30-second review with staff.
  4. Stay in Detailed when you need the audit trail back to QuickBooks.

What this format changes — and what it keeps

Kept from QuickBooks: every account name, order inside Payroll, and all totals exactly as reported. Changed: only presentation, so the statement is faster to read month to month.

01 — AT A GLANCE

KPI header

Income, gross profit, expenses, and net with margins up top. QuickBooks buries net at the bottom; here you know the month before you scroll.

02 — CONTEXT

% of income on every line

$2,609.56 for Lab means more as “6.3% of income.” Percentages make months of different sizes directly comparable.

03 — PRIORITY

Expenses sorted by size

QuickBooks sorts alphabetically. This sorts largest-first (Payroll → Lab → Supplies…) so attention goes where the dollars are.

04 — SECOND VIEW

Grouped management roll-up

People / Clinical / Facility / Admin / Other — five buckets for a quick review, with the detailed QuickBooks lines one tap away.

05 — VISUAL WEIGHT

Share bars

A thin bar next to each line shows relative size instantly, without turning the statement into a chart.

06 — READABILITY

Tabular numbers, clear totals

Mono numbers align on the decimal, subtotals are tinted, and Net Income gets a double-weight rule — printable and phone-friendly.

QuickBooks default

  • Alphabetical expense list
  • Amount only — no % of income
  • No summary header or margins up top
  • Payroll subtotal inline, easy to miss
  • Best for: filing, accountant handoff

This proposal

  • Same data, sorted by dollar impact
  • Amount + % of income + share bar
  • KPI strip + grouped roll-up included
  • Same account names — maps 1:1 back to QB
